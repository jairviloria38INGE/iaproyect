Paisa Heart & Innovation

Traditional Cuisine

Hearty Bandeja Paisa (tender beans, ground beef, crispy pork chicharrón, fried egg, ripe plantain, avocado, and corn arepa).

Musical Rhythm

Witty improvised Trova verse battles, classical tango salons, and the global epicentre of contemporary Latin urban music.

Flower Festival

The world-famous Desfile de Silleteros in August, where mountain farmers carry intricate giant floral sculptures on their backs.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Cartagena de Indias

Situated on Colombia’s Caribbean coast, Cartagena is South America's colonial crown jewel. Its miles of fortified ramparts shelter romantic cobblestone squares, vibrant street life, and bougainvillea-draped balconies.

Must-Visit Landmarks

  • The Clock Tower Gate & Old Town Plazas
  • San Felipe de Barajas Fortress
  • Rosario Islands archipelago day cruises
  • Getsemaní artistic quarter & street murals

Culinary Highlights

  • Crispy fried red snapper with plantain chips
  • Sweet coconut rice with titoté raisins
  • Arepa de huevo (crispy corn cake stuffed with egg)
  • Chilled coconut lemonade
Natural Wonder

Santa Marta

The oldest surviving Spanish settlement in South America. It serves as the gateway to the world-renowned Tayrona National Natural Park and the sacred peaks of the Sierra Nevada.

Must-Visit Landmarks

  • Tayrona National Natural Park (Cabo San Juan)
  • Expedition trekking to the Lost City (Teyuna)
  • Taganga fishing bay and scuba diving spots
  • Quinta de San Pedro Alejandrino estate

Local Gastronomy

  • Cayeye (savory green plantain mash with cheese)
  • Coconut-infused seafood stew
  • Shrimp cocktail with lime and Caribbean spices
  • Exotic juices: corozo, passion fruit, and lulo
City of Eternal Spring

Medellín

Internationally lauded for its transformative urban innovation and warm climate. Surrounded by emerald Andean ridges, Medellín is a beacon of culture, entrepreneurship, and vibrant nightlife.

Iconic Highlights

  • Botero Plaza with 23 monumental sculptures
  • Comuna 13 outdoor escalators & street art tour
  • Arví Ecotourism Park reached via Metrocable
  • El Poblado cafes and botanical gardens

Paisa Flavors

  • Full Bandeja Paisa with avocado and plantains
  • Sweet corn arepas topped with fresh cheese
  • Antioquian beef empanadas with homemade chili
  • Award-winning specialty pour-over coffee
Cosmopolitan Capital

Bogotá D.C.

Standing at 2,600 meters on an Andean plateau, Bogotá captivates travelers with its grand pre-Columbian gold collections, bohemian quarters, mountain outlooks, and thriving gastronomic innovation.

Points of Interest

  • Monserrate Sanctuary (3,152m above sea level)
  • The Gold Museum (world's largest pre-Hispanic display)
  • Historic La Candelaria neighborhood
  • Bolívar Square and the National Capitol

Capital Flavors

  • Traditional Ajiaco chicken and corn soup
  • Hot chocolate served with farm cheese & bread
  • Tolimense breakfast tamales
  • Crispy wafer obleas drizzled with caramel
